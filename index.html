Each column changes one stat while holding the others at your current values. The first number is the total you'd need; the number in brackets is how much more than you have now. Intellect can't make casting infinite, since a bigger bar only delays OOM.

Model: mana is checked at the start of each cast, casting regen starts after your first cast finishes, and the full out-of-casting regen never kicks in because you never stop for 5 seconds. Regen is treated as continuous rather than 2-second ticks, so real results can be off by a cast.
